# N03 source licensing checklist

Official source: https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N03-2026.html

The official page describes N03 as open data under CC BY 4.0 and also states that secondary use may require an application to the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan under the Survey Act. Check the precise conditions for redistribution, derivative data, and public map use **before** importing or republishing derived polygons. Attribution and source year must be preserved.

Do not interpret the availability of a download as blanket permission to redistribute a transformed polygon dataset. If permission is uncertain, use official municipal-code lists and non-redistributive validation instead. The official N03 release uses JGD2011; verify coordinate transformation and resulting SRID rather than assuming the raw source is EPSG:4326.
